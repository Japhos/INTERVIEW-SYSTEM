Overview

Quick Guide

  1. Add Courses being interviewed for.
  2. Add Candidates and assign each to a course.
  3. Add Users / Interviewers accounts so each panelist can log in.
  4. Review the Scoring Criteria rubric (defaults to 100 marks).
  5. Interviewers log in, choose a course, then a candidate, and submit marks. A candidate can only be scored once per interviewer — resubmission is blocked automatically.
  6. View the ranked, printable list under Results & Print, or audit/reset individual scores under Score Records.
